What is Pomofocus?

Pomofocus is a customizable pomodoro timer that works
on desktop & mobile browser. The aim of this app is to
help you focus on any task you are working on, such as
study, writing, or coding. This app is inspired by
Pomodoro Technique which is a time management
method developed by Francesco Cirillo.
